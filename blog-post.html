How I run product discovery without slowing the team down

discovery process research

Placeholder intro. Discovery has a reputation problem: engineers hear the word and picture two weeks of stickies while the roadmap burns. It doesn't have to be that way — continuous discovery is a cadence, not a phase.

1. The weekly touchpoint — the one that surprises everyone

Placeholder. One hour, every week, three customers. Same three questions. The compounding effect after eight weeks is impossible to argue with, because you stop debating opinions and start quoting people.

✦ Rule of thumb: if a discovery activity doesn't change a decision within two sprints, cut it.

2. Instrument before you interview

Placeholder. Analytics tells you where the product leaks; interviews tell you why. Doing them in that order halves the interview count you need.

-- the three queries I run before any interview cycle
SELECT step, COUNT(*) AS drop_offs
FROM onboarding_funnel
GROUP BY step ORDER BY drop_offs DESC;
