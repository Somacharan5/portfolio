Origin.The Story Behind

This site began as a faithful study of a portfolio I admired. I wanted a digital home that felt 'alive' — so instead of a template, it was rebuilt from the studs with a focus on craftsmanship.

The design language, layout system, and motion feel are a tribute to the original creator's work, rebuilt from scratch and refilled with my own story, content, and product-flavored details.

This iteration is a statement on how I believe the web should be: performant, accessible, and aesthetically refined.
